Our Story

Five Generations of Millers

Miller Hardware opened its doors in Harrison back in 1931, and five generations of Millers have kept them open ever since.

We're a small-town hardware and lumberyard at heart — the kind of place where we know your name when you walk through the door, and often your family too. Whether it's a box of nails or a full building project, we're set up to deliver the big jobs just as well as the small ones.

Every day we get to meet new neighbors as Marion and Boone County keep growing, and we're growing right along with them. We'd be honored to earn the business of every family, farm, and contractor in the area — one relationship at a time.

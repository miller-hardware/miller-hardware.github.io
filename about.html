Our story

Family owned, locally rooted.

Miller Hardware Company was established in 1931 and is still owned by the Miller family. It is a multi-generational local business, and it runs like one.

We keep two stores, in Harrison and Yellville, and we are affiliated with True Value. That is how we can order items from its catalog for you, and it is why a quick call is worth making before you drive anywhere for something unusual.
